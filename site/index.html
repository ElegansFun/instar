Instar is a persistent world of Drosophila melanogaster first-instar larvae on an agar plate. Every larva runs the whole-brain connectome published by Winding et al. in 2023: the same — neurons, the same — synaptic connections. It senses, crawls, feeds, molts and breeds on its own. Each one is an NFT on Solana, in the Instar collection: buying mints it into your wallet, resale moves it, death burns it.

1What is real

What the connectome gives, and what it does not.

The wiring is real. The dynamics are ours. Every claim on this page is meant to survive that sentence.

Winding and colleagues reconstructed the brain of a first-instar larva from electron microscopy: every neuron, every chemical synapse, with the number of synaptic contacts between each pair of cells. That dataset is the entire nervous system of every animal in this dish. The engine loads its — nodes and — weighted edges verbatim from the canonical file below, whose Merkle root is quoted in section 6.1

The dataset does not give neuron dynamics, neurotransmitter identities for most cells, neuromodulation, the ventral nerve cord, or muscles. So the engine supplies the same integer threshold unit for every neuron (a potential, a refractory counter, a fired flag), assigns a small set of roles from the paper's own annotations (which sensory neurons are olfactory, gustatory, thermosensory; which descending neurons leave the brain toward the nerve cord or the feeding apparatus; which cells form the ring gland), and treats the rest of the body as a few equations. A larva here is descended from the real larval connectome. It is not a real larva, and it does not behave exactly like one.2

What the wiring does do is measurable, and it is measured, not asserted. The figure below is filled from probe runs over the checked-in engine; each number carries the horizon it was measured over.3

Roles the engine reads from the census
ORN, gustatory (external, pharyngeal), thermo (cold, warm), visual, gut, respiratory from the sensory annotations; mechano, nociceptive, proprioceptive from ascending neurons; descending to VNC (left, right, unpaired) for locomotion; descending to SEZ for feeding; ring gland for ecdysone.
Neuron model
One integer unit for all — cells. Propagation is event-driven along the census edges. Fixed point 16.16; xorshift64* random source; no floating point anywhere in the engine.
Hash
Every — ticks the engine hashes population, positions, genomes and every neuron's state (FNV-1a, 64 bit). The world posts that hash to its account on chain; this page recomputes it and reads the account back.

3The dish

An agar plate with weather.

The plate is a disc of radius — cells inside a —×— grid. Outside the disc is wall; a one-cell rim runs inside it. Every cell carries food (yeast), moisture, and one of seven substrates. Light follows a day of 16,384 ticks; temperature drifts on a period of 131,072 ticks around the 24 °C baseline the thermosensory neurons compare against. Both are pure functions of the tick, so they are the same in every mirror.

Reproduction
Ecdysone accumulates from ring-gland firings. A larva with energy ≥ 45,000 and ecdysone over threshold, with a partner within 4 cells holding ≥ 12,000, produces a child by crossover with 1–6 signed mutations to edge weights. The child spawns adjacent.
Sensing
Probes read two cells ahead of the head. Olfaction is the change in food odour between ticks (klinotaxis); external taste is food underfoot; pharyngeal taste is what was actually eaten last tick.
4The market

Where the lamports go.

Money lives as lamports on the world account, a program-derived address on Solana. Four pools are tracked inside it: each larva's vault, the metabolism that sets how many animals the plate supports, the pool that pays living animals, and credits owed to people. The program keeps one invariant on every instruction: the account's balance above rent covers all four.

Buying, listing and withdrawing happen on the dish page, where you have an account.

Custody
There is no wallet adapter. An account here is a Solana keypair the world process holds for you, encrypted under your PIN. You can withdraw everything to any address at any time. That is a deliberate design and a real trust assumption; read it as one.
If the operator disappears
After 90 days without an operator action anyone may begin wind-down; keepers reclaim their vaults as credit; after 180 days everything above rent sweeps to the recovery address. No lamport is stranded by design.
5The record

Each larva is a Creature account: id, parent, generation, birth tick, genome hash, keeper, vault, price, status. It is never closed. Reading the hash column
“verified” means this page reached that tick itself, hashed its own engine memory, got the same 64 bits the journal shows, and then read the World account from the cluster and found the same bytes there. “matches journal” means the journal agrees but the account has not been read for that epoch: the post is still pending, the chain has since moved to a newer epoch, or the RPC could not be reached from this browser. “before join” means the page joined the world from a snapshot after that epoch; add ?full=1 to the address to replay all history from genesis and check every epoch.
